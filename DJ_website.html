About

The short version

Darren Luistro is a DJ who treats a set like a story worth telling slowly. Equal parts crate-digger and host, he reads a room the way old radio hosts used to — a little soul here, a little groove there, always building toward the moment everyone's moving without quite noticing when it started.

Whether it's an intimate bar set or a full room on its feet, Darren's approach stays the same: good records, played with care, in the right order.

Biography

How it started

Darren's first record player was a hand-me-down, and the first crate of vinyl came from a relative's closet — mostly soul and funk, a little scratched, all of it formative. What began as curiosity about how those records were made turned into hours spent learning to blend one into the next, chasing the feeling of a transition nobody notices until it's already happened.

From bedroom sessions to friends' parties to his first real bar gig, Darren built a reputation the old way: by showing up prepared, reading the crowd honestly, and never playing down to a room. Along the way his sound widened — house, disco, R&B, a little bit of everything with groove in it — but the throughline never changed. It's still about the record, the room, and the moment they meet.

These days Darren plays a mix of club nights, private events, and the occasional late-night radio hour, always looking for the next crate worth digging through.
